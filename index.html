About Me

I am a Severo Ochoa Postdoctoral Fellow at the Instituto de Ciencias Matemáticas (ICMAT) in Madrid, Spain, where I work under the supervision of Piergiulio Tempesta.

My research focuses on integrable and superintegrable systems, particularly their behavior in the presence of magnetic fields, using the framework of Haantjes and Nijenhuis geometry. I study separation of variables, differential geometry approaches to classical and quantum mechanics, and geometric structures underlying integrable systems.

I completed my PhD in Mathematical Physics at the Faculty of Nuclear Sciences and Physical Engineering, Czech Technical University in Prague in September 2024, under the supervision of Libor Šnobl and Antonella Marchesiello. My doctoral thesis, which received the Rector's Award for Excellent Doctoral Thesis 2025, focused on integrability and superintegrability in the presence of magnetic fields.

Alongside my research, I am engaged with AI safety. I volunteer with PauseAI Czech Republic, where I write the weekly newsletter Pozastavení nad AI on developments in AI policy and safety.

Research Interests

  • Integrable and superintegrable systems
  • Haantjes algebras and symplectic-Haantjes geometry
  • Separation of variables
  • Systems with magnetic fields and velocity-dependent potentials
  • Differential geometry in mathematical physics
  • Classical and quantum mechanics
